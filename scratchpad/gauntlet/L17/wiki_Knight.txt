The Knight is a {{Rarity|Common}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked in the [[Training Camp]]. It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, and ground [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] with high hitpoints and moderate damage. The Knight takes the appearance of a man who has brown eyes, a blond-colored mustache and eyebrows, and wears chain mail and a chestplate, a team-colored sash and a belt, and brown shoes with metal plates on them, while wielding a saber as his weapon. A Knight card costs 3 {{Icon|I=Elixir}} to deploy.

==Modifiers==

{{Quote|+200% hitpoints & -50% speed}}

==Strategy==
{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Knight Card}}
*Having high health, Knight has a good statline for his cost, being able to survive 1 hit from all damaging [[:Category:Spell Cards|spells]] at equal levels. He is able to to block a strike from [[Lightning]] or a vine from [[Vines]], allowing him to protect weaker [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] on defense, like [[Musketeer]], [[Bomber]], [[Mega Minion]], [[Wizard]], [[Flying Machine]], [[Witch]], [[Dart Goblin]], [[Hunter]], [[Princess]], [[Electro Wizard]], [[Firecracker]], [[Electro Dragon]], [[Ice Wizard]], [[Phoenix]], [[Bandit]], [[Magic Archer]], [[Night Witch]], [[Cannon Cart]], [[Fisherman]], [[Mother Witch]], [[Archer Queen]], or [[Little Prince]].
*The Knight is quite weak to air troops, as he is unable to target them and does not move quickly. For example, [[Minions]] and [[Bats]] can easily defend against a Knight for a low [[Elixir]] investment. He's still useful for tanking hits from them if there are other cards with lower hitpoints, especially those who can attack the air troops.
*[[Electro Wizard]] works well with the Knight, since his attack can stun and reset attackers to cause them to re-target onto the Knight. However, this combination is weak against large swarms like [[Skeleton Army]], so it’s recommended to supplement this push with spells or area damage troops.
*The Knight has a great synergy in particular with [[Goblin Barrel]] [[Battle Decks|decks]]. His low cost and relatively high hitpoints allow him to easily be used to tank opposing [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Tower]] hits for the Goblin Barrel, making the spawned [[Goblins]] much harder to take down if [[Zap]] or [[Giant Snowball]] is their opponent's only small spell. On defense, he is the primary tank in the deck which helps against larger pushes. However, [[Barbarian Barrel]] or [[The Log]] will still counter the Goblin Barrel even if the Knight is tanking for them, so it is not recommended to use him in a combo when these cards are in the opponent's hand early on.
**The Knight is a somewhat decent counter to a Goblin Barrel if the player has no other counters, because he one-shots Goblins. He should be placed on the side of the player's Crown Tower closest to the edge of the Arena so he can take out the bottommost Goblin (the one the Tower targets last); this allows for more effective damage distribution between the player's units and often results in less damage taken. Nonetheless, the Knight is not a substitute for another card that can fully stop the Goblin Barrel.
**Using this strategy, the Knight can similarly be paired with [[Graveyard]], although this is a more costly synergy at 8 Elixir.
*If he is paired with fast building-targeting units like [[Battle Ram]], [[Hog Rider]], [[Ram Rider]], or [[Wall Breakers]], the Knight will be pushed closer to the Crown Tower for extra damage or towards a defending unit, allowing him to attack the defending unit earlier, while tanking hits for those units to deal significant damage to the Crown Tower.
**This can be countered by separating the Knight from the unit with a kiting unit like [[Ice Golem]], then addressing the building-targeting unit with a building, such as [[Cannon]] or [[Tesla]], or a high damage dealer unit that can kill the distracted Knight later, like [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.]], Prince, [[Lumberjack]], or [[Mighty Miner]].
*Splash units like Bomber, Wizard, Princess, Firecracker, [[Goblin Demolisher]], [[Bowler]], Magic Archer, and [[Executioner]] can hit both the Knight and units behind him, leaving them weakened. It's preferred to keep said units at a distance so that they don't get defeated, unless the splash troop has high health, so they can tank the Knight's hits and deal damage to him and the other unit(s) at the same time. [[Wizard/Evolution|Evolved Wizard]], Bowler, and [[Executioner/Evolution|Evolved Executioner]]'s knockback abilities will also grant them extra survivability.
**To counter those splash cards (especially higher health ones), the player should deploy their Knight at a considerable distance from those troops, allowing support troops to be deployed on top of them without being defeated. Troops with a linear Area of Effect or a troop spawning mechanic require extra assistance. The Knight can also be placed on top of the troop if the support troops are ranged.
*For just three Elixir, the Knight is a good [[Goblin Drill]] tank. His high health for a low cost make the player respond to both him and the Goblin Drill, because only answering the Goblin Drill would still leave the Knight threatening Crown Tower chip damage, while only answering the Knight allows the Goblin Drill to survive for its entire lifetime and spawn far more Goblins. Unless a healthy splash damage troop is played, a neutral or positive Elixir trade is likely to occur. The strategy is even better with the [[Goblin Drill/Evolution|Evolved Goblin Drill]], given that, because of its submerging ability, the Drill won't be able to efficiently tank for its spawned Goblins, making Knight far more required in this situation.
*Knight's average hit speed and his inability to hit multiple targets makes him somewhat weak to swarms. However, since his damage per hit is high enough to one-shot Goblins, swarms with a lower troop count should be placed further away from him so that less of them get defeated during their deployment.
*Knight has enough health and damage along with a low enough cost to counter low hitpoint or low damage support troops like Musketeer, Wizard, Witch, Electro Wizard, Ice Wizard, Magic Archer, Executioner, Cannon Cart, Mother Witch, or Archer Queen. With the help of a Crown Tower, the Knight can win several one-on-one interactions against cards like Mini P.E.K.K.A., [[Dark Prince]], [[Prince]], [[Giant Skeleton]], Lumberjack, [[Goblin Machine]], [[Golden Knight]], or [[Skeleton King]].
**With support cards like [[Archers]], Spear Goblins, or Dart Goblin, the Knight will be able to take out more dangerous cards such as [[P.E.K.K.A.]], [[Mega Knight]], Bowler, [[Boss Bandit]], Mighty Miner, and [[Monk]], or combinations like Musketeer + Valkyrie. Although the Knight might not survive, the support troops used will be ready for a counterpush or even just chip damage on the opposing tower.
**The Knight is able to take down a lone Mega Knight by himself. The player should let them cross the bridge, travel a few tiles, and put the Knight close to the Mega Knight, making him unable to jump. This gains enough time for both the Crown Tower and the Knight to defeat the Mega Knight without tower damage. Alternatively (especially if the Mega Knight is supported), the Knight can be placed closer to the center without letting the Mega Knight travel far. This makes the Mega Knight jump, but both towers will target him, defeating him either way.
*A Knight can be used to bait the enemy's swarms or tank counters, allowing a more threatening tank like a [[Giant]] or a [[Golem]] to have an easier time on offense.
*He can be supported with Goblins, [[Spear Goblins]], [[Skeletons]], or [[Guards]] to quickly deal high damage on offense or defense. If the opponent tries to eliminate one of the two units, the overall cheap cost of the combo often gives the player enough time to back up the other one. When defending against this push, area damage and air troops are the most efficient way to clean it up.
*A lone Knight will deal great damage to a Crown Tower by himself, taking around half of its health before being defeated. However, this is usually not a recommended play as an unsupported Knight can be defeated rather easily on the opponent’s side of the arena. Tanks like Valkyrie or cheap troops like Skeletons can pull him towards the center and negate most, if not all, of his damage. Due to his low cost, however, it usually won't be a bad play, and it will even serve to scout the enemy's deck.
*Knight can be used as a cheap tank for tanking an enemy [[X-Bow]]. If the player knows the X-Bow user cannot block him in time, he will do a fair amount of damage to it for 3 Elixir. However, if the X-Bow user does have enough resources to back it up, it is more effective to place him further back and to rely on another troop to deal damage to the [[:Category:Building Cards|building]], as he will otherwise be in range of the opponent's Crown Tower and take extra damage. A similar principle works with the [[Mortar]], with its [[Mortar/Evolution|Evolution]] being a more favorable situation, because the Knight would also tank for the spawned Goblins.
**On the other hand, he can be used as a tank for Mortar or X-Bow due to his low upfront cost and high hitpoints. He will act as a shield for enemy attacks that would be used against the building, while also doing damage to them.
**Similarly to Mortar or X-Bow, a Knight can be deployed at the bridge as a surprise play to protect a [[Princess]] played at the bridge, allowing her to deal extra chip damage. He won't protect her from spells or splash cards, but the former choice does not deal with the Knight, and the latter choice usually evens out the 6 Elixir investment. On the contrary, the Knight is a good counter to a Princess at the bridge, especially if the player is saving their spell(s) for a deadlier card, like Goblin Barrel.
*For a generally cheaper Elixir cost, the Knight can tank for spawner troops, like Witch, [[Furnace]], Night Witch, and Mother Witch. He is healthy enough to allow them to spawn multiple waves of troops, dealing chip damage to enemies around while threatening the Crown Tower decently well. Witch and Furnace can deal with swarms, while Night Witch is better for high damage output, and Mother Witch serves to directly threaten the Crown Tower. The downside to using him instead of building-targeting troops that usually accompany those cards is that the Knight doesn't directly go towards the Crown Tower, and using [[Rocket]] and [[Lightning]] to counter the push is more effective, as the support troop would be defeated while the Knight would be severely crippled.
*The Knight can counter a lone [[Sparky]] that has crossed the bridge for a positive Elixir trade. This is because, at the same level, the Knight can survive one Sparky blast, and with the help of a Crown Tower, destroy her before she can attack again.
*On defense, Goblins and Spear Goblins can be placed in the center of the allied side of the arena in order to defeat the Knight for a +1 Elixir trade. This applies to any [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troop]].
*Knight can be used to waste [[Ronin]]'s deflections because of his high health and low damage output if needed, allowing other melee troops to damage him. His moderate attack speed can also chip Ronin's health decently enough.
*Knight is able to tank the [[Elite Barbarians/Evolution|Evolved Elite Barbarians]]' spears, allowing support troops to attack them unscathed.
*Knight can be deployed to counter a lone [[Wizard/Hero|Hero Wizard]] and [[Bowler/Hero|Hero Bowler]]'s abilities for a -1 Elixir trade, but avoiding Crown Tower damage. If they're allowed to cross the bridge (and especially if the Knight is in the same lane), then they can be countered for a +4 Elixir trade instead.
**Similarly, the [[Valkyrie/Hero|Hero Valkyrie]] and [[Berserker/Hero|Hero Berserker]]'s abilities can be stopped by placing a Knight for a neutral or -1 Elixir trade. The Knight will also leave them vulnerable enough for the Crown Tower to finish them up, resulting in a +2 or +4 Elixir trade.
*In terms of his role as a mini-tank, the Knight can be substituted by other more expensive mini-tanks, that can specialize in additional roles. The main advantages of the Knight compared to the rest of those cards is his low Elixir cost for the amount of hitpoints that he has and his decent hit speed. However, depending on what the player wants, those cards can serve as an alternative to the Knight. Additionally, some of them can be used in the same deck as the Knight if the player wants to make use of their non-mini-tank roles.
**If the player wants a fast mini-tank that deals significant damage at once, the player should pick Mini P.E.K.K.A. or Prince. Both of them have higher damage than the Knight, are faster, and the latter is also bulkier, can jump over the river, and has a charge, allowing him to move even faster, however they cost more Elixir, and attack slower, making them weaker to swarms. Generally, they should be picked over the Knight when their offensive capabilities are more necessary.
**If the player also wants their mini-tank to have area-damage, they may want to use Valkyrie, Dark Prince, Royal Ghost, or Skeleton King, in their deck. They all can handle swarms more effectively thanks to their splash, and Skeleton King is also bulkier than the rest, while Dark Prince and Royal Ghost are faster. However, Knight still has a lower Elixir cost than the others for his bulk.
**An even cheaper mini-tank is Ice Golem, costing 1 less Elixir than Knight, and also comes with death damage and a slow effect. However, unlike Knight, he is completely passive, which makes him less effective at defending larger pushes. In exchange, his passivity can allow him to kite troops in the other lane. Knight is better used to defend against larger pushes and setting up for a counterattack, while Ice Golem is better to be used against lone troops.
**The Knight paired with Archers can replicate the same effect of [[Rascals]] for a slightly more expensive cost. However, Archers have more hitpoints than Rascal Girls, allowing them to survive an equal level The Log, and their lower individual costs allow the player to defend more efficiently to several attacks (i.e, they are not forced to store 6 Elixir for using on a single attack, unlike what happens with the Rascals, costing 5 Elixir).
**[[Battle Healer]] can be used in place of Knight for increased longevity. Despite having lower hitpoints, her healing capabilities allow her to stay alive for longer, raising her chances for a counterattack. However, she has significantly less damage, so she should only be picked if the player wants to make use of her healing capabilities. As such, she should not be used over Knight in X-Bow or Mortar decks, as the healing has no effect on buildings.
**If the player wants to use a {{Rarity|Champion}} in their deck as their mini-tank, they may want to use Golden Knight, Skeleton King, Mighty Miner, or Monk to replicate Knight's role. The Golden Knight plays very similarly to the Knight, costing +1 Elixir in exchange for a faster hit speed, with Dashing Dash costing +1 Elixir to pressure the opponent and clear swarms. Skeleton King's Area of Effect can be good at dealing with swarms, while Soul Summoning can easily overwhelm opposing troops for +2 Elixir, making him better on decks with swarms. Monk costs +2 Elixir, but has more health, attacks faster, and can knock all troops back, with Pensive Protection reflecting projectiles back to the sender for +1 Elixir alongside a 65% damage reduction. However, if the player wants to use other {{Rarity|Champions}} or {{Rarity|Heroes}} in their deck, then the Knight is the clear choice.

==Trivia==
*The word "''Knight''" comes from the German "''Knecht''", meaning "servant or vassal". Originally, knights were associated with mounted warriors, later being considered a class of lower nobility, until eventually, knighthood had become associated with the ideals of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chivalry chivalry], a code of conduct for the perfect courtly Christian warrior. Often, a knight was a vassal who served as an elite fighter or a bodyguard for a lord, with payment in the form of land holdings.
**The Knight is 1 of 20 [[cards]] associated with nobility, with the others being [[Royal Recruits]], [[Royal Giant]], [[Royal Delivery]], [[Tower Princess]], [[Royal Hogs]], [[Dark Prince]], [[Prince]], [[Raging Prince]], [[Mega Knight]], [[Princess]], [[Royal Ghost]], [[Spirit Empress]], [[Super Knight]], [[Dagger Duchess]], [[Royal Chef]], [[Golden Knight]], [[Skeleton King]], [[Archer Queen]], and the [[Little Prince]].
*The Knight is 1 of 7 cards that are already unlocked for the player when they start playing the game, the others being [[Minions]], [[Archers]], [[Arrows]], Tower Princess, [[Giant]], and [[Fireball]]. Despite this, the game recognizes that those cards are unlocked in the [[Training Camp]].
**In the tutorial, the Knight is the first card that the player is told to use.
*The Knight is the 1 of the 4 cards have both Evolution and Hero form, the others being [[Valkyrie]], [[Musketeer]] and [[Wizard]]. 
*The Knight is one of the first [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] cards to be featured in a Trainer's [[Battle Decks|deck]], alongside the Minions, Archers, [[Spear Goblins]], [[Goblins]], [[Bomber]], [[Skeletons]], [[Barbarians]], [[Minion Horde]], [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.]], [[Musketeer]], Giant, Valkyrie, [[Wizard]], [[Hog Rider]], [[Baby Dragon]], [[Skeleton Army]], [[Witch]], [[P.E.K.K.A.]], Prince, and [[Balloon]].
*The Knight is one of the first troops to be featured in a [[Loading Screens|Loading Screen]], alongside the Goblins, Skeletons, and Prince.
*The Knight is one of the first troop cards to receive a card render, alongside Archers, Goblins, Bomber, Skeletons, Barbarians, [[Bats]], Royal Recruits, [[Skeleton Barrel]], [[Rascals]], Musketeer, Giant, Valkyrie, Wizard, [[Flying Machine]], Hog Rider, Royal Hogs, [[Dart Goblin]], [[Zappies]], [[Elixir Golem]], Baby Dragon, Skeleton Army, Witch, P.E.K.K.A., Prince, Balloon, [[Giant Skeleton]], [[Goblin Giant]], [[Hunter]], [[Electro Dragon]], [[Wall Breakers]], [[Bowler]], [[Cannon Cart]], Mega Knight, [[Ram Rider]], [[Miner]], Royal Ghost, [[Ice Wizard]], [[Magic Archer]], [[Bandit]], [[Lava Hound]], [[Night Witch]], and [[Fisherman]].
*The Knight is 1 of 10 troops that have been featured under the [[Ruler Ability#Special Delivery|Special Delivery]] [[Ruler Ability|ruler ability]], the others being Skeleton Barrel, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Musketeer, Wizard, P.E.K.K.A., Giant Skeleton, [[Golem]], [[Sparky]], and Royal Ghost.
*From his description, it is implied that the Knight and the Barbarians are distant cousins, due to their physical similarities, especially the color of their hair and mustaches. Coincidentally, both the Knight and the Barbarians are voice acted by [https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1423395/ John Mondelli].
**However, in the Dutch language setting of the game, the Knight is said to be the Barbarian's nephew.
**According to John Mondelli, the Knight has a French accent.
**The [[Electro Wizard]] and the [[Lumberjack]] are also voice acted by John Mondelli.
*The Knight is featured in non-Clash games. More specifically:
**The Knight is featured as [https://brawlstars.fandom.com/wiki/Pins?file=Clash_Royale_Pin-Knight.png a pin in Brawl Stars], resembling the [https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/Emotes?file=Cheer_Knight.png Knight Cheer Emote].
**While not available as a character, the Knight's sounds, along with Barbarians', Wizard's, and the Dark Prince's, were featured in the [https://beatstar.fandom.com/wiki/Clash_Royale_Theme Clash Royale Theme] [https://beatstar.fandom.com/wiki/Songs song] in [https://beatstar.fandom.com/wiki/Main_Page Beatstar].
*When the Knight is defeated, his metallic sword falls to the ground, with the blade slightly buried into it. The other units that drop items upon death are Rascal Boy, [[Mega Minion]], Dark Prince, [[Executioner]], Mega Knight, Sparky, Fisherman, Super Knight, Golden Knight, and the Little Prince.
*The Knight is the subject of certain cosmetics in the game. More specifically:
**The Knight is the subject of a few [[Clans|Clan]] badges.
**The Knight is the subject of 2 [[Emotes]].
**The Knight is the subject of a few [[Battle Banners|Battle Banner]] frames.
*The Knight was featured in the winning decks of multiple Clash Royale Tournaments. More specifically:
**The Knight was featured in the winning deck of the [https://www.youtube.com/live/psZXKWHkKkE?si=PbjvbZt8stghz6eJ King's Cup II Tournament], by a player called Surgical Goblin.
**The Knight was featured in the winning deck of the [https://www.youtube.com/live/cmBlEI-aDoo?si=UoD1Sit2YvQR19xs Clash Royale League 2019 Tournament], by a player called Surgical Goblin.
**The Knight was featured in the winning deck of the [https://www.youtube.com/live/h3IOFTfT8Sk?si=wTF1FekCifpSULn- Clash Royale League 2020 Tournament], by a player called Ruben.
*In the [https://youtu.be/zWW1dtcsDGk?si=Ytadsvv1hpz23v9y Card Mastery intro video], the Knight's Damage Dealer [[Card Mastery]] task was shown to require the Knight to deal 77,000 damage. However, it was raised to 77,500 in-game.
*The Knight's {{Rarity|Common}} [[C.H.A.O.S]] Modifier "''Truly of the Round Table''" is a reference to the knights of the round table present in Arthurian Legend. The round table was used to indicate that King Arthur and his knights had equal authority and say in resolving matters.
*The Knight has had balance changes that were planned for it, but were altered or delayed. More specifically:
**On 4/10/2022, the Knight was supposed to receive a 3% Hitpoints increase, but it was [https://royaleapi.com/blog/season-40-balance-wip pulled back] until the [[Version History#2022 Quarter 3 Update (26/10/2022)|2022 Quarter 3 Update]].
**The initial balance change for Knight on the [[Version History#Balance Changes (7/2/2023)|7/2/2023 Balance Update]] was a 6% Hitpoints increase, but it was lowered to 3% [https://royaleapi.com/blog/season-44-balance-wip last minute].
*Before the Knight's card release, the Knight's Hitpoints were decreased by 7%.
