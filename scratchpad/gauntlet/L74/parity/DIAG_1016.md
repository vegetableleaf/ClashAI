# S2 step 2: why the worst-agreeing fights disagree (diagnosis, 2026-10-16)

Runtime for every number here: royalesim **0.1.35**, the PyPI `cp310-abi3-win_amd64` wheel unpacked on the laptop
(`PARITY_SIM_PATH`, the same wheel `PARITY_SCOREBOARD.md` used), card table `client160402017_20261006`. "Before" numbers
come from the pod full run (`FIGHTS35`, Linux 0.1.35) or from the HEAD `fights.py` (a2b1422) run locally. Tools and raw
outputs: `diag1016/` (scripts) and `output/diag1016_*` (gitignored runs).

## Verdicts

| mechanic | verdict | one-line cause |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor ranking itself (own Log / Skeletons / Hero IW "worst") | **H (method)** | A 12 s window holds about 5 plays. The failing check is mostly tower damage, and it is mostly caused by other bodies in the window, not by the anchor card. The anchor's own bodies agree at 0.91 to 0.94. |
| Own spell cast tick | **H, fixed** | A tap made short of elixir is played live only when the elixir arrives. The harness cast every spell at tap + median delay, so 20% of Logs landed 0.4 to 1.1 s early in the SIM. |
| Own spell level | **H, fixed** | A spell is never seen as a body, so it got the side's modal level 16. The live Log is level 15: it deals 390, against 429 at level 16. |
| Shield on setup bodies | **H, fixed (partly)** | A setup spawn gets a full shield even when the live unit has already lost hp, which can only happen after the shield broke. |
| Card-less rows read as towers | **H, fixed** | Moving card-less reader rows (293 or 915 hp) took a tower's id and hp. This produced impossible negative tower damage and the Elixir Golem group's 6,739 and 2,617 tower errors. |
| Log rolling laws (`ROLLING_HIT_SHAPE`, `ROLLING_HIT_SWEEP`, `ROLLING_SPARES_LEAPERS`, width, timing) | **not S** | After the fixes, 91% of the bodies either side hits are hit by both. Hit timing median 0 ticks. At the lateral box edge, 57 of 58 bodies agree. The residual is position cascades and hidden state. |
| Skeletons formation, survival and death | **not S** | Members end on exactly the same points live and SIM. The 150 millitile first-frame error comes from a 1-tick phase in the birth spread. Survival agreement is 0.951. |
| Hero IW (slow, splash, ability) | **not S (U for the hp gap)** | The anchored IW agrees on survival in 106 of 113 cases. Fights with an ability press agree as well as fights without one (0.643 vs 0.652). The SIM IW takes 8% less damage, and the extra live drops look like hidden opponent damage (untested). |
| Elixir Golem death spawns | **H (towers) + U (small layout residual)** | Its tower errors were the card-less-row artefact (strict mean 2,057 → 185). When the parent's death matches, the children's offsets agree to about 60 millitiles at the median, with a few at 230 to 315. |

**No S patch is proposed in this ticket.** None of the four mechanics shows a SIM law disagreeing with live once the
harness artefacts are removed. Proposing a fork change on this evidence would be fitting noise. The next ticket's
candidates are listed at the end.

## 1. The anchor ranking is mostly not about the anchor card (H, method)

### Evidence: decomposition of failing strict fights (pod FIGHTS35)
| group | n | agree | fights with a focus (anchor) survival mismatch | fights failing tower damage | alive (focus) agreement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| own Log | 108 | 0.565 | 10 | 40 | 0.907 |
| own Skeletons | 107 | 0.636 | 6 | 30 | 0.944 |
| own Hero IW | 104 | 0.683 | 9 | 28 | 0.913 |

- **Fights are not isolated.** 95 of 108 Log fights, 95 of 107 Skeletons fights and 90 of 104 IW fights have a later
  own play in the same window. Only 6 or 7 per group are "solo".
- **Overlapping windows share errors.** One Tornado that drags a Pekka and a Ghost to the king (`142821@858`) fails
  every fight whose window holds it: here a Log fight and the next Skeletons fight.
- **The ranking follows frequency.** Impact = fights/match × (…). Log, Skeletons, IW, Tesla and X-Bow are the five
  cards played most often (about 1.2 per match each). Their outcome agreement, however, sits near the base rate
  (strict 0.698). Only own Log (0.565, about 2.8 SE below the base rate) clearly underperforms, and §2 shows that was
  harness timing and level.

### Evidence: what makes a tower fail
These are the failing towers of 342 strict fights in the four groups, replayed after fixes 2 to 4 (`diag1016/towers.py`,
`towers_an.py`). The classification is by the bodies and hp drops at the first tick where the gap exceeds 150.

| class | share (n = 102) | example |
|---|---|---|
| same attackers, different hits (attack phase / cooldown / target timing) | 47% | `170420@3050`: a 76 hp Prince dies to a tower shot live at t+148; in the SIM the shot lands 4 ticks later and the charge hits for 1,138 |
| own X-Bow on tower: hit count or start time (98 per hit) | 22% | `183748@841`: the SIM X-Bow stops after 2 hits; an Electro Wizard set up with no target zaps it in the SIM only |
| attackers differ (an earlier survival or position cascade) | 21% | `174501@3379`: the SIM Royal Giant kills our 458 hp Tesla, so the Royal Hogs walk straight instead of diagonally to the Tesla |
| SIM-only hit with no SIM enemy within 9 tiles (long-range targets: Princess, X-Bow) | 6% | `145128@1021`: an opponent Princess at 10.4 tiles shoots our tower in the SIM only |
| live-only hit with no live enemy (invisible opponent spell) | 4% | drops of 269 and 243 with nobody near |

27 of 102 diverge within 2 s of t0, which is setup state. Every class is hidden per-unit state or an invisible action,
not a SIM law.

### Proposed harness and scoring change (next ticket, not done here)
- **Per-card verdict on the card's own bodies.** Use focus alive agreement plus death time, which is already the
  `alive` column. Score tower damage per attacker, or end the window at the next deployment. Do not use the 12 s
  whole-window outcome.
- **Answers to the lead's three questions:**
  - *Tolerant outcome:* most tower failures are one or two hits (98 to 400 hp), so a 10% rule passes a large share.
  - *Noise ceiling:* every class above is hidden state, so they are plausibly within the ceiling.
  - *Logging that would shrink it:* per-unit target id and attack phase (or a fired-shot event), the Tesla hidden flag
    each frame, and opponent spell and area-effect entities each frame (see §6).

## 2. Own spell cast tick: tap with elixir short (H, fixed)

**The mechanism.** Live, a card tapped before its elixir is there is played only when the elixir arrives:
- All 215 own troop plays that registered 12 or more ticks after the match's median tap-to-spawn delay were short of
  elixir at the tap (`elixir − cost` median −0.40).
- Only 4.9% of the 1,835 on-time plays were short.
- Extra delay ÷ deficit = 45.5 ticks per elixir at single elixir (n = 178, IQR 41.5 to 48.3), 22.9 at double (n = 126)
  and 22.4 in overtime ticks 3600 to 4800 (n = 37) (`diag1016/delays2.py`).

**Why it mattered.** A spell has no body to time it by, so `fights.py` cast it at tap + median delay.

| own Log casts with a both-hit body (`logcmp.py`) | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| per-cast SIM − live first hit within ±2 ticks | 0.651 | 0.777 |
| SIM ≥ 8 ticks early | 50 | 7 |
| SIM ≥ 8 ticks late | 12 | 13 |

All 48 of the early casts had a predicted wait above 5 ticks. After the wait, 44 of 48 land within ±5 ticks.
`142821@858`: the Tornado was replayed 17 ticks early and dragged a Pekka and a Ghost to our king, 766 tower hp of error.

**The fix.**
- `fights.elixir_wait()`. A spell tick is now tap + delay + (cost − elixir) × 45.5 / {1, 2, 3}, with double elixir from
  tick 2400 and triple from 4800.
- The triple divisor is unmeasured: n = 14 gave 11.9.
- Hero IW presses use the ability's `mana_cost` of 2.
- The events carry `elixir_wait`. Tests: `test_spell_tick_waits_for_elixir`.

**Residual.** 13 casts are 8 or more ticks late. Their cause is unknown (U); the elixir reading at the tap may be stale.

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* a 0.4 to 1.1 s early Log changes which bodies it hits, so a tolerant outcome does not absorb it.
- *Noise ceiling:* it was a harness error, not noise.
- *Logging:* log the tick each play is accepted. The elixir drop is already in `confirmed`, but at decision resolution.

## 3. Own spell level (H, fixed)

- **Live:** the Log's single-frame drops on enemy troops are 390 (465 drops; `diag1016/spell_dmg.py`).
- **SIM probe:** Log 355, 390 and 429 at levels 14, 15 and 16. So the live Log is level 15.
- **What the harness did:** it gave every spell the side's modal level, 16, so the Log dealt 39 too much per body and
  moved kill thresholds (Princess, Blowdart Goblin and others died in the SIM only).
- **The fix:**
  - `Engine.spell_damage()` probes the first hit of a cast on a still 10⁶ hp Golem.
  - `spell_levels()` picks the level whose damage is the most frequent exact live drop, with at least 2 drops.
  - Inferred levels: Log 15, Tornado 16 (134 per tick; live 134 × 275), Rocket 16.
  - Matches with no exact drop keep the modal level.
- Tests: `test_spell_levels_pick_the_level_whose_damage_is_seen`, `test_spell_drops_window`.

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* survives a tolerant outcome when it decides a kill.
- *Noise ceiling:* not noise.
- *Logging:* none needed.

## 4. Shield on setup bodies (H, fixed partly)

**Probe.** A Royal Recruit setup spawn at 300 of 547 hp keeps a full 240 shield, and so does a Dark Prince. Live, any
lost hp means the shield is gone.

**The fix.**
- `run_fight` zeroes the shield of a setup body below its max hp and flags `setup_shield_broken`.
- `161106@3794`: the 322 hp Recruit is now killed by the Log in the SIM too.

**Left over (hidden state).** A shield can break with hp still full: the next 796-of-796 Recruit took 390 from the Log
live and nothing in the SIM. Royal Recruits are still the second-largest Log mismatch (11). Flagging full-hp shielded
setup bodies as hidden state is a one-line option; I did not add it to the clean blockers.

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* 240 hp per Recruit survives a tolerant outcome.
- *Noise ceiling:* the full-hp part belongs in the noise ceiling, since shield hp is not public.
- *Logging:* log shield hp per body if the reader can see it.

## 5. Card-less rows read as towers (H, fixed)

**The bug.**
- `read_match` called every `card_id == -1` row a tower and named it by x.
- Moving card-less rows exist: hp 293 (214 frames), 915 (118) and others, 491 of 182,352 frames in all.
- They took the id of a real tower (T0R, T0K) and replaced its hp. Example: `091131`, where T0R reads
  293 → 2905 → 2500 and T0K reads 293.
- Result: 12 pod fights show negative live tower damage. Among them are the Elixir Golem group's worst two (6,739 and
  2,617).

**The fix.** A card-less row is a tower only on one of the six tower slots (±600 millitiles). Otherwise it stays a body
with `card_id = -1`, which flags `setup_unmapped_body`, as before for unknown bodies. Test:
`test_cardless_row_off_a_tower_slot_is_not_a_tower`.

**Rescore of the 5 affected matches** (pod → laptop, all fixes):

| set | outcome agree | fixed / broke | mean tower err | opp Elixir Golem |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| strict | 0.532 → 0.617 | 4 / 0 | — | tower err 2,057 → 185, agree 0.200 → 0.600 (n = 5) |
| core | 0.507 → 0.603 | 20 / 0 | 441 → 229 | — |

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* survives a tolerant outcome.
- *Noise ceiling:* not noise.
- *Logging:* a reader-side tower flag, or the slot index, on tower rows.

## 6. Log hit set (not S)

Paired ground bodies near each own Log, same matches before and after (`logset.py`, `logcmp.py`). A hit is an exact
390 drop, a 390 drop plus one common other hit, or a kill within 70 ticks.

| | hit by either side | both | live only | SIM only | disagree |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (HEAD harness, level 16) | 1,126 | 1,019 | 50 | 57 | 0.095 |
| after | 1,088 | 992 | 43 | 53 | 0.088 |

The earlier "15% of hit bodies differ" counted no kills, and the level mismatch broke exact-drop detection, so it was
inflated. Timing of the bodies hit by both: median 0 ticks; within ±2 ticks for 693 of 992.

**Residual mismatches after the fixes (n = 92, `log_geo.py`):**

| class | n | share |
|---|---|---|
| live and SIM body more than 250 millitiles apart at the hit (an earlier position cascade) | 64 | 70% |
| same place, not at an edge | 27 | 29% |
| within 300 of the lateral box edge | 1 | 1% |

- **Same place, not at an edge (27):** Royal Recruits (hidden shield, §4), Witch and Dark Witch spawned skeletons
  (spawn timing), and Tesla (hidden underground, one in each direction).
- **Lateral edge:** 57 agreeing bodies sit within 300 of the edge too, so the box half-width 1950 + radius
  (`ROLLING_HIT_SHAPE`) matches live.
- **Royal Hogs (12):** these are cascades. In `174501@3379`, the hogs are spared live because they leap diagonally
  toward our Tesla, which the SIM had already lost. In `155341@2546` and `171105@2320`, the setup hogs were mid
  river-leap and the SIM respawns them walking. Jump state is hidden, and `setup_snapped` applies.
- No case contradicts `ROLLING_SPARES_LEAPERS = spared_mid_leap`. I also found no case that tests it cleanly (U).

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* the 8.8% residual is body-level; a ±0.5 s and 10% outcome absorbs part of it.
- *Noise ceiling:* plausibly within the noise ceiling (positions inherited from hidden state).
- *Logging:* jump or leap state, the Tesla hidden flag, shield hp, and per-frame projectile positions (the Log is
  already in `raw_projectiles`, but only at decision ticks).

## 7. Skeletons (not S)

**Formation** (`diag1016/skel.py`, 162 plays with 3 + 3 members). The final member offsets equal the SIM's in every
traced play, for example (−1001, −500), (0, −500), (1001, −500).
- 40% of fresh Skeletons have a first-frame error of exactly 150. That is one tick of the 150 per tick birth spread:
  live and SIM are one frame apart in the slide from the centre, and both reach the same points within 4 to 6 ticks.
- This is the mid-gap birth tick (10 Hz frames). It is not a law (H, harmless).

**Survival.** Survival agreement 0.951 (n = 2,171 strict); dies live only 40, SIM only 66; death time error 0.1 s
median. Anchored strict fights with a Skeletons survival mismatch: 6 of 107.

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* yes, it passes a tolerant outcome.
- *Noise ceiling:* the remainder (1/4-tile 0.813, later retargeting) is plausibly within the ceiling.
- *Logging:* per-unit target id.

## 8. Hero Ice Wizard (not S; U for the hp gap)

- **Anchored strict fights, all fixes** (`diag1016/iw.py`; `iw_strict.txt`):
  - 113 IWs: dies live only 5, SIM only 2.
  - Mean damage taken: live 352, SIM 324 (8% less in the SIM).
  - Drop values seen live and not in the SIM: 52 (8×), 147 (6×), 279 (5×).
- **Is 279 a hidden opponent spell?** 279 matches the opponent's Zap at level 15 (75 × 3.72), and also Barbarians or
  Electro Dragon. Untested; the test is to look for simultaneous equal drops on 2 or more bodies with no visible
  attacker.
- **Core vs strict:** in core fights (opponent spells allowed), IW deaths are live-only 191 vs SIM-only 39. So hidden
  opponent spells, which the strict filter catches only when they are projectiles, are the main IW killer the SIM
  never sees.
- **Ability:** strict fights with a press agree 0.643 (n = 84), without one 0.652 (n = 655). IW survival mismatch is
  8 of 103 with a press and 43 of 740 without. The press replay is not a measurable source.
- **History:** the HANDOFF's "A/B3 Ice Wizard: SIM +3.3 vs live −12.5" is a policy-level result. It is not explained
  by any IW mechanic measured here (untested link).

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* survives a tolerant outcome only where it decides a death.
- *Noise ceiling:* plausibly within the ceiling (invisible spells).
- *Logging:* opponent area-effect entities (Zap, Earthquake, Poison, Freeze, Rage, Lightning) every frame. The
  decision record already carries `model_effects`, but only at decision ticks.

## 9. Elixir Golem death spawns (H + small U)

- **Tower errors:** the card-less-row artefact, see §5.
- **Child layout** (`diag1016/egolem.py`; matches 154121, 091131, 161106). For parent deaths matched within ±4 ticks and
  800 millitiles:
  - Child offsets from the parent's last point agree to about 60 millitiles at the median, n ≈ 22 children,
    hand-tallied.
  - Exact in 5 cases, for example (−750, 0) and (764, 120).
  - Outliers of 230 to 315 (`4598`, `4645`).
- **The 424 millitile first-frame error in `PARITY_SCOREBOARD.md`** mostly comes from the parent dying elsewhere or at
  another time: parent position error of 150 to 757 in those fights.

**Lead's questions.**
- *Tolerant outcome:* the residual is below a 1/4-tile for most children.
- *Noise ceiling:* plausibly within it, except the outliers (U).
- *Logging:* none beyond the per-unit target and attack-phase logging above.
