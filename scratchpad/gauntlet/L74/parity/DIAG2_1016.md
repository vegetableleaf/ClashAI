# Body-survival misses for own Skeletons, Hero Ice Wizard and Tesla (2026-10-16, DIAG2)

**Question (lead).** The strict scoreboard has own Skeletons at 0.782, own Hero Ice Wizard at 0.848 and own Tesla at
0.869 normalised. The gap is mostly bodies that die live and survive in the SIM, or the other way round. What causes
each failing fight: the harness (H), a SIM law (S), or hidden state the reader does not see (U)?

**Answer, in short.**

- Five harness causes were found and fixed in `fights.py`. Two of them are scoring artefacts and three are setup gaps.
- On a 37-match laptop subset (K = 8), strict overall normalised goes from **0.896 [0.853, 0.931]** to
  **0.914 [0.878, 0.943]**, and agreement goes from 0.778 to 0.813.
- The remaining failures for these three cards are mostly hit-timing noise that the ceiling reproduces (U), plus a
  residue that is still unresolved.
- **No SIM law is established.** One candidate, the Mega Knight dash trigger, rests on a single live jump. It is listed
  with a test plan, not with a patch.

Runtime: royalesim 0.1.35, the laptop wheel (`PARITY_SIM_PATH`), table `client160402017_20261006`. Diagnosis scripts and
raw outputs are in `.foreman/scratch/diag2/` (local only).

## 1. Ice Wizard slow at fight start (item 1)

Code changes in `fights.py`:

- `SLOW_BUFFS` and `IW_SLOW_MS` are new constants.
- `Engine.slow_ids()` probes the SIM buff ids. The result is `hero_cold` 66, `hero` 67 and `base` 1, the same ids as
  IW_SLOW_1016 §2. The ids are not hard-coded.
- `set_buff()` writes into the flat 4-slot buffs list. It uses the first free slot that the entity owns.
- `iw_shots()` infers a slow from projectiles.
- In `start_state` there are two branches, and the edits are applied in `run_fight`:

| source | rule | validation |

Only the freeze (999,999 sentinel) stays unset. | unit test `test_start_state_tesla_charge_jump_and_freeze` |

The latest hit wins. | Against phase-2 truth at every fight t0 of 6 logs: **60 matched, remaining ms within 50 ms in 60/60** (median −50). 12 truth-only (splash victims, which a projectile cannot show). `phase2_buffs` now also reads the phase-3 captures (`re_phase3/p3_*`). These carry the same rows for `133158`, `134401`
and `135037`. `PARITY_BUFFS=<file>` replaces the capture directories. The file is made by
`fights.py buffs --out <file>` and holds names and remaining ms only, with no addresses.

**Pod finding (H).** In the pod run every summary has `phase2: false`. The pod has no reader captures, so no phase-2
stun or slow was ever applied there. The pod command in §6 ships the buffs file.

**Reading the subset numbers:**

- The ceiling rises with the fixes for two reasons. Censoring applies to SIM-vs-SIM pairs too, and seeded spawners
  remove a spread the perturbations did not model. So normalised scores move less than raw agreement does.

  established. The direction holds for all six cards listed.
- The full cohort needs the pod run (§6).

**Notes on the unresolved classes:**

- **"Killer in place, hit timing" is symmetric.** The SIM killer stands where the live one does but hits earlier or
  later. Across the three cards, the SIM is earlier in 21 body pairs and later in 22. That is the shape of hidden
  attack phase, not of a biased law (a missing slow would make the SIM consistently early).
- **These killers are not covered by the ceiling.** In 0/8 perturbed replays do the failing fights reproduce, because
  the ceiling redraws only t0-board bodies that have history. New plays inside the window, parent-made riders and
  proxies are never redrawn. Read this class as U (phase), unproven.
- **Unseen-spell detection is unreliable beyond the Poison case.**
  - Same-gap multi-kills with no splash unit in reach do not coincide with any seen spell on ext logs (0/6).
  - The "no opponent in reach" hits on ext logs were rider or spear projectiles.
  - So only the Poison signature is counted as U-confirmed. A strict-set blocker built on this detector would exclude
    fights on outcome; it was not built.

## 7. Limits and what is not done

- Old logs get no IW slow seed. A seed from hp drops (an IW-damage drop with an IW in reach) is the next step; 71 of
  the 389 fights are exposed at most.
- Spawner seeding needs a child wave seen in the last cycle. Without one the state is left at the setup default (28
  `setup_spawner_unseen` and 8 `setup_spawner_overdue` on the subset). Spawn-speed slows on spawners are not modelled.
- The ceiling still does not redraw new plays, riders or proxies. The "killer in place" residue cannot be put inside
  the noise without that.
- 121354 and 121550 show the same Mega Knight jump one tick apart. They appear to be one match logged twice, which the cohort would double-count (not checked further).
