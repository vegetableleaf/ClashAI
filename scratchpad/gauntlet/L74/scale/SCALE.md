# SCALE.md -- scaling curve v0 (plan1026 Pillar 4c, ticket S2)

Imitation GenModel at 1.5 / 2.2 / 5.0 / 10.6 / 20.2 / 35.1 / 52.0 M parameters on the existing pro set. Labels: **(a)** measured, **(b)** plausible but untested, **(c)** contradicted. All numbers come from `results/*/eval.json` (made by `scale_eval.py`) and the trainer logs; the full tables at the bottom are generated by `aggregate.py`.

## Setup (identical for every size unless marked)
- Data: `gen_dataset_v32_fv5.npz`, sha256 `29ecefa9d3025a51d7d4e4cbf67ad15a6fe79a9fd7b222b394dd391e0bc76530` (identical on all 4 pods). Train = `split==0`, 3,168,599 rows (12,377 steps/epoch at bs 256). Val = `split==1`, 349,264 rows; the split is by replay-tag hash (`dataset_gen.py:436`), so no replay straddles train/val.
- Recipe = `pipeline.train_gen` at HEAD cb026b0, unmodified (`--d/--layers` already exist, so no wrapper): AdamW lr 3e-4, wd 0.01, OneCycle pct 0.05, bs 256, grad-clip 1.0, mirror p 0.5, bf16 autocast, **4 epochs = 49,508 steps**, seed 0, grid lattice, feature_version 5, `--inputs-only-arm` (no Rocket-context weighting; so m1p5 is not byte-comparable to gen_v32_s0, which used weight 4.0, but it is the same d128 x L4 architecture, 1.52 M params). d_c 64, 4 attention heads, dropout 0.1 for all. Launcher: `run_size.sh NAME D LAYERS [SEED] [EXTRA]`.
- Checkpoint = best epoch by cell_tile_top1 on the trainer's fixed 30k val sample (epoch 4 for every healthy run).
- **Held-out numbers use `heldout_clean` = all 319,264 val rows MINUS that 30k selection sample** (so epoch selection cannot leak). v3val (12,237 rows, S1's instrument) and a fixed 100k TRAIN subset (eval mode: no dropout, no mirror) are reported beside it. NLL = mean cross-entropy: cell over play rows (lattice label), card over play rows (hand pointer), wait over wait rows, gate = BCE over all rows, value over all rows. `NLL total` = cell + card + 0.5 wait + gate + 0.5 value (the trainer's loss weights). Ticket heads: gate/timing = gate (+ wait), card = card, cell = cell.
- Grid (d x layers): 128x4 / 160x4 / 224x6 / 320x7 / 384x10 / 512x10 / 576x12. Width and depth grow together (not separated).
- Pods: A 32657 (m1p5, m5, m20, m2_seed1 and ALL latency passes), B 33920 (m2, m10, m5_seed1, m35), 33308 GPU1 (m52), 30408 (m52lr, ran alone on its GPU).

## Result headline
1. **(a) Held-out NLL improves steeply to ~5 M, slowly to ~20 M, then total stops improving.** Total held-out NLL: 1.52 M 5.423 | 2.16 M 5.338 (seed1 5.361) | 4.96 M 5.286 (seed1 5.270) | 10.6 M 5.247 | **20.2 M 5.234** | 35.1 M 5.242 | 52.0 M 5.270 (lr 1.5e-4). Step gains (2.2 and 5 M as the mean of two seeds, 5.349 and 5.278): 1.5 to 2.2 M -0.07, 2.2 to 5 M -0.07, 5 to 10.6 M -0.03, 10.6 to 20.2 M -0.013, 20 to 35 M +0.008.
2. **(a) Seed noise** (2 seeds at 2.2 M and 5 M): total NLL differs by 0.024 / 0.017, cell NLL by 0.019 / 0.015, tile top-1 by 0.56 / 0.80 pp. So 10.6 to 20.2 M (total -0.013, cell -0.016) is **inside seed noise**; 5 to 10.6 M (-0.03 vs seed mean, -0.04 vs s0) is at the edge; 1.5 to 5 M is clearly real. Single seed for 1.5 / 10.6 / 20 / 35 / 52 M.
3. **(a) Heads differ.** The cell head keeps improving to 35 M (2.912 at 20 M, 2.898 at 35 M; noise-sized step; tile top-1 24.7% to 25.6%). Gate NLL flattens at ~10 M (0.3925, 0.3904, 0.3912). Card and wait get WORSE from 20 M up (wait NLL 1.164 at 2-5 M, then 1.173 / 1.187 / 1.210 at 10.6 / 20 / 35 M; card 0.851 at 20 M, 0.860 at 35 M). Value NLL rises steadily (0.966 to 0.977).
4. **(a) Overfitting grows monotonically with size** on this 3.5 M-row set. Held-out minus train total NLL (eval mode): +0.157 (1.5 M), +0.195 (2.2 M), +0.258 (5 M), +0.332 (10.6 M), +0.413 (20 M), +0.486 (35 M); the wait-head gap goes +0.09 to +0.30. Train total NLL keeps falling (5.27 to 4.76) while held-out total bottoms at 20 M. The plan expects this data limit; whether 17.8 M HF actions move the knee right is (b, untested; that is curve v1).
5. **(a) The 52 M model at the shared lr 3e-4 DIVERGED in epoch 3** (selection-sample cell NLL 3.019 at ep2 to 3.393 at ep3; train loss 5.31 to 5.99). Its selected checkpoint is epoch 2 (`m52_s0`, held-out total 5.459: under-trained, NOT a fair 52 M point). Following the iterate-don't-abandon rule I re-ran it at **lr 1.5e-4 (`m52lr_s0`; the ONLY deviation from the shared recipe)**: stable, total 5.270, worse than 20 M and 35 M, gap +0.437. So at 3.5 M rows 52 M is not better than 20 M (a, one seed). Whether a tuned lr or more data changes that is (b). LR was not tuned for any other size (b: 20-35 M might also gain from a lower lr).
6. **(a) Top-1 is noisier than NLL.** Held-out tile top-1: 21.9% (1.5 M), 22.9 / 22.4 (2.2 M s0/s1), 22.9 / 23.8 (5 M), 24.5 (10.6 M), 24.7 (20 M), 25.6 (35 M), 23.8 (52 M lr). Card top-1: 61.6, 62.2/62.0, 63.2/63.2, 63.4, 63.6, 63.5, 63.4%. m35 has the best tile top-1 yet a worse total NLL than 20 M; use NLL for the decision.
7. **Latency (RTX 4090, forward only, eager PyTorch, no compile; the host CPU was shared, so bs1 is launch-bound).** bs1 fp32 p50: 5.6 ms (2.2 M) to 7.5 ms (20 M), 7.4-7.9 ms at 35-52 M; going 1.5 M to 20 M costs about +1.6 ms at batch 1. bf16 autocast is SLOWER at bs1 (6.6-11.4 ms). bs1024 grows roughly linearly with size; bf16 p50: 22.7 ms (1.5 M), 52 (5 M), 94 (10.6 M), 200 (20 M), 225 (35 M), 329 (52 M). Train GPU peak at bs 256 including the on-GPU dataset: 3.5 GB (1.5 M), 7.6 (10.6 M), 11.4 (20 M), 14.6 (35 M), 18.9 GB (52 M).

## Recommendation (where gains flatten)
- **Learner (4a): 10-20 M.** Past ~10 M the held-out gain is inside seed noise; 20 M has the lowest total NLL but already a +0.41 train/held-out gap and 2x the bs1024 cost of 10.6 M. If the 4a latency gate is tight, 10.6 M (d 320 x 7) is the knee. At batch 1 the forward is 6.6 ms vs 5.8 ms at 1.5 M on the 4090, so the forward is not the 79 ms live decision cost (consistent with the plan's profiling expectation). **RTX 5050 latency was NOT measured here.**
- **Big opponent-teacher (35-52 M): not worth building on THIS data.** 35 M has the best cell head but worse card / wait / value; 52 M is no better than 20 M. Revisit on HF-scale rows (curve v1).
- **Rollout budget concern (a).** bs1024 bf16 forward is 93.8 ms for 10.6 M = about 10.9k decisions/s per 4090 in eager mode (45k/s at 1.5 M). The plan's 4b figure (~200 M policy calls/h = 55k/s) therefore needs about 5 GPUs of 10.6 M policy, not 2, unless the forward is sped up (torch.compile, CUDA graphs, fused attention, int8: all (b), untested). Model forward only; observation building is not included.
- Closed-loop play strength per size is NOT measured (offline NLL only). The plan's closed-loop checks for the chosen sizes are still owed; offline agreement/NLL is not play strength (repo history: agreement rose while play fell).

## Caveats
- Single seed except 2.2 / 5 M. Wall times are NOT clean: runs shared a GPU two at a time. Clean-ish throughput from `train.out`: about 2.4-2.7k rows/s for 1.5-5 M (shared), 1.5k alone at 20 M, 1.0k at 35 M (partly shared), 0.75k at 52 M.
- Not the CLXIII architecture (fv6 adds a projectile-target branch, 1.76 M); these are fv5 GenModels.
- Latency was taken with other tenants' CPU jobs on the host; bs1 includes launch jitter. `eval peak MB` = peak GPU memory of the latency pass (model + one batch + the dataset on GPU).
