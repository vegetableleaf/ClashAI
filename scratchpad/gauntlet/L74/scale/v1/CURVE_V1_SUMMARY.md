## Does more data move the knee?  (a for the numbers, b for the reading)
1. **No knee up to 20.8 M in v1 (a).** Gains per step on heldout_hf: m2->m5 0.058, m5->m10 0.059, m10->m20 0.051; on heldout_v32: 0.051, 0.054, 0.045. Seed noise (|m10 s0 - m10 s1|, ONE pair) is 0.022 / 0.017, so each step is 2.3-3.2 noise units. Curve v0 flattened there: m10->m20 gain 0.013 (inside its seed noise), gaps +0.33 (10.6 M) and +0.41 (20.2 M).
2. **The overfit gap is 4-5x smaller (a):** 0.07-0.09 at 11-21 M against +0.33 / +0.41 in v0. One epoch of 13 M rows does not overfit these sizes, which is what stopped v0.
3. **But the control says the data is not what lifts the pro-row score (a, ONE size, ONE seed).** At m10 on heldout_v32 (the pro rows both curves share):
   - v0 m10 (legacy trainer, fv5 plain, v32 only): 5.2467
   - control m10 (v1 recipe, v32 train rows only, 51,000 steps = 4.1 epochs of the 3.17 M v32 rows, the same count as v0): 5.3101
   - v1 m10 (v1 recipe, mix = v32 + HF): 5.3146 (mean of 2 seeds 5.3229)
   - recipe/family effect (control - v0, same data): **+0.063 (worse)**; data effect (v1 - control, same recipe): **+0.005 (no gain, inside the 0.017 seed noise)**; total +0.068.
   So on the pro-row distribution the HF rows add nothing at m10, and the v1 recipe (fv6 + memory v2, step trainer, one OneCycle over 51k steps, selection on HF held-out) is worse than v0's by 0.06. The HF gain is on the HF distribution: heldout_hf 5.312 (v1) vs 5.956 (control, which never saw HF rows; different distribution, so this -0.64 is "HF rows teach HF behaviour", not a quality ranking).
4. **Reading (b):** the continuing gains in v1 are consistent with "more distinct rows, fewer epochs remove the overfit that capped v0", but the control shows the extra rows themselves do not improve pro-row NLL at 11 M; the v1 curve's steady slope could equally be the longer-schedule recipe at larger sizes. A control at m20 (same recipe, v32 only) would separate them; not run. So: knee moved right in v1 (a), cause NOT established (b); do not read it as "HF data moves the knee".

Note on the control: its checkpoint is the one selected on HF held-out total = step 35,000 of 51,000 (a v32-only model that never trains on HF is selected by HF performance, so an earlier step wins); its last checkpoint (`opp_ctl_m10_s0_last.pt`, on the pod) was not evaluated, (b) it may score differently on v32. Its training rows are the same v32 train rows as v0's.

## Decision for the successor size (b)
- Per-step gains show no knee to 20.8 M under the v1 recipe; the compute cost is mild: training 1.6 h (2.5 M) / 1.7 h (5.4 M) / 2.3 h (11.1 M) / 3.2 h (20.8 M) on one shared 4090; bs1 decide-path (eager) 8.5 / 9.5 / 9.5 / 10.6 ms and one CUDA graph 1.4 / 1.6 / 1.9 / 2.3 ms on the 4090 (a; `CURVE_V1.md` table 6; bit-identical to eager on the captured row for all four).
- So size is not the live-latency constraint up to 21 M IF the graph design (ONE_FORWARD.md) is built; the constraint is closed-loop strength (`base_ice` 38.96% vs CLXIII 69.58%), which this curve does not address.
- Seed noise rests on one pair at m10; the sizes m2/m5/m20 have one seed each.
