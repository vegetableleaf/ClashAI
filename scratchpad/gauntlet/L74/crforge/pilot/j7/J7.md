## Play ticks: how the 2 candidates are used (a)
- **Primary = `lo`.** It equals the record's `tick`, the fights.py convention, on 11,226 of 11,226 commands:
  - 9,463 have lo = hi (phase-3 stamp);
  - 1,719 have a 1-tick gap and tick = lo;
  - 44 sit mid-gap.
- **Ceiling = the same engine re-run with every play at `hi`.** The frame gap is the only play-tick freedom the inputs leave.
  The "ceiling seq" column is agreement between lo and hi on the same engine. It measures sensitivity only; it is not a
  perturbation ceiling like FIN15's 0.834.
- crforge: a play is queued with given = run - 20 and runs on `run`. Its first object appears at run + 1 (LagProbe 13/13).
- RoyaleSim: the play is issued with `step(cmd, 0)` at tick `run`, as fights.py does.

## Drivers
- **crforge** (`J7Run.java`, in package `org.crforge.core.battle.match`; built against ad8ef3a). The build follows SmeRun /
  ReplayBattle.build:
  - seed 0, two player-data draws, `startLadderMatch` with words and slot flags;
  - then `Hand.rearrange` to each side's deal. RNG-free decks draw nothing after the deal (G0), so the seed changes nothing else;
  - abilities are queued on their giving tick, on the unit of the side's last *placed* play of that card.

  Modes:
  - `gate`: the match's own gates.
  - `force`: before the step that runs a play, the card is swapped into a hand slot and elixir is filled. This is what
    `fights.play()` does for RoyaleSim.
- **fin15 / fin16** (`fin_run.py`, using fights.Engine).
  - Engines:
    - fin16 = wheel b5c35101 via `PARITY_SIM_PATH` (provenance 3f567607 clean), with `PARITY_TABLE_1019=1` (table
      `cards-160402017-20261019.1`);
    - fin15 = `ROYALE_RUNTIME=fin15-linux` (305aaafc clean, table 10-06).
  - Build: `reset` from tick 0 with the full decks, levels, forms from the slot flags, tower levels and the tower troop.
  - Each play uses fights.py's `play()` forcing: hand slot 0, mana full, evo charge from the observed form.
  - It is **not** fights.py's whole-match J7. That one starts from the live board at the first frame and adds its own
    harness laws (throw templates, form oracle and so on). Here both engines get the same plan from tick 0.
- **Grading**: `replay_metrics.j7()` unchanged. Live rows are matched to the engine state at the same tick. CIs are
  `replay_metrics.boot` (2,000 match resamples). Paired differences use per-match differences, resampled the same way.
- `test_j7.py`: the scorer gives full agreement when the engine run equals the truth, and catches a fall that is late or
  missing (passes).

## Notes and concerns
1. **Trivial agreements (a).** 43 of 138 matches have no live tower fall. An engine that fells nothing agrees on those. The
   95-match subset with a fall gives the same gain (+0.274), so the result does not rest on them.
2. **Misfit matches (a).** On the 25 opponent-deal misfits, gate mode collapses (0.280): the extractor's missed or invented
   opponent plays get refused and cascade. Force mode survives them (0.560). These 25 are where extraction, not the engine,
   limits the score (b).
3. **The ceiling is below the target (a).** crforge's lo-vs-hi self-agreement is only 0.855. A 1-tick move of the 1,763
   non-exact plays changes the crown sequence in 14% of matches. crforge force raw / that = 0.89.
4. **Refusal counts.**
   - The "refused" columns in SCORE.md include plays that never ran because the engine's match had already ended (crforge
     gate_4 = OVER; fin `never_run_after_end`). Those are an outcome difference, not an input loss.
   - Real refusals:
     - crforge force: 10 abilities with no placed unit, 2 not placed, 1 queue error, 1 play before tick 21;
     - fin16: 67 `ability:NO_HERO`, 42 `ABILITY_SPENT`, 47 `OUT_OF_TERRITORY`, 20 `NO_DEPLOY`, 2 `WATER`;
     - 6 fin matches dropped the Hero Electro Wizard form (the SIM cannot load it).

     fin's placement refusals of points crforge accepts are an engine difference, not yet attributed (b).
5. **crforge faults (a).** 2 matches (`20261026_174147`, `_225213`) stop on crforge's own `UnsupportedOperationException`:
   an evo Goblin Drill play after a placement-refused one, "not modelled". This happens in every crforge arm. They are
   graded on the state frozen at the stop: both still agree on the crown sequence.
6. **King wake (b).** crforge's king counts as awake once `isInactive()` turns false after it was first inactive (tick 1
   precedes the inactive tag). Live uses kind 13. Graded at ±100 ticks, as J7 does.
7. **RNG-only for now.** RNG matches (Arrows, Princess, Hunter, Skeleton King: 89 more in `inputs_post1019`) were not run.
   They need seeds (A2b-3).
