ClashAI — Model & Architecture Change Log
=========================================

Tracks changes to the agents' models, learning architecture, and the
vision / training pipeline. Dates are local (file history + working notes).
Newest entries are at the bottom.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-07-23  —  Project inception: the scripted bot  (trol/)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- First bot: a rule-based agent with a DQN scaffold (trol/src/clashbot/).
- Screen capture, window calibration, and template-matching vision.
- Mouse controller, and a home/match/end state machine for navigating menus.
- DQN + online-learning scaffolding (dqn.py, learning.py, learning_state.json).
- Outcome: workable for scripted behaviour, but hard to make it genuinely
  "play". Led to the rethink below.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-07-24  —  New direction: the learning bot  (real/)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Started a second agent built around IMITATION LEARNING first, then RL
  fine-tuning (real/src/clashrl/).
- Recording pipeline: capture the game region + mouse clicks to
  data/sessions/<timestamp>/ (record.py; video.mp4 + events.jsonl + meta.json).
- Config-driven capture / observation / action space (config.py, config.yaml).
- Match-outcome detection: read win/loss (crowns per team) off the results
  screen automatically (outcome.py).
- Card knowledge base imported from open card data (card_import.py, cards.yaml)
  so rewards/placement can reason about card stats and ranges.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-07-25 (morning)  —  RL infrastructure, the policy model, reward shaping
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tower & King HP reader: a small CNN digit OCR (tower_hp.py + hp_digits.npz)
  reads tower/king HP off the screen to drive reward shaping.
- Action space + grid placement helper (actions.py); menu-navigation
  diagnostics (diagnose.py, the `diag` command).
- POLICY MODEL (model.py, PolicyNet): a shared CNN trunk over the downscaled
  arena image, concatenated with a hand multi-hot, feeding two heads:
    * card head  — which card IDENTITY to play (an evolved card is its own
      identity, since it plays differently — not the tray slot, which cycles)
    * cell head  — which grid cell to place it on
- Behaviour cloning (train_bc.py) pretrains the policy to copy your recorded
  plays (card head trained with a mask over the cards actually in hand).
- RL fine-tune (train_rl.py): a DQN wrapper adds a wait/play GATE head over the
  shared embedding; target network + replay; epsilon-greedy with elixir-aware
  exploration.
- Live RL environment (env.py) + reward shaping (reward.py):
    * +take enemy tower, -lose own tower, +win / -loss
    * defend-your-towers (tower-HP based), troop-defeat + patience
      (enemy-mass detection)
    * spell rewards scaled by the troops HIT / KILLED; Rocket auto-aims the
      weaker enemy princess tower
    * range-aware, center-biased DEFENSIVE placement for troop cards
    * tornado-onto-your-own-king "tank / activate" reward (king-HP based)


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-07-25 (afternoon)  —  Deck change, defensive doctrine, "next card" input
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- DECK CHANGE: replaced Evolved Musketeer with ROYAL DELIVERY. Deck identities
  went 10 -> 9; the policy is rebuilt on the next `train-bc`.
- Royal Delivery reward: a delayed area spell cast on your own half — rewarded
  for the enemy mass it lands on, and more for the mass it kills.
- King-activation gating: the "tornado onto your own king to tank/activate it"
  reward now only applies while BOTH your princess towers still stand (once one
  falls, the king is auto-active and the mechanic is off).
- DEFENSIVE DOCTRINE: any non-Rocket, non-cycle card played in the enemy half is
  penalised. The Rocket is the only sanctioned offensive card (weaker princess);
  cheap cycle cards (Ice Spirit, Skeletons) are exempt so they can cycle freely.
- ARCHITECTURE CHANGE — "next card" input: PolicyNet now also takes the NEXT
  (preview) card as a one-hot, via a new next_fc branch (shared embedding grew
  from 256+32 to 256+32+16). Threaded end-to-end through the live env, live play,
  dataset labeling (dataset.npz gains a `nexts` array), BC training, and the RL
  replay/DQN. This lets the policy plan its cycle (e.g. hold a cheap card to line
  up the counter that's coming). Requires re-labeling + retraining to take effect.
- Next-card VISION: the preview is a smaller, blue-tinted rendering that does NOT
  reliably match the in-hand card templates, so it uses its OWN template set
  under templates/next/. `hand-templates` now also extracts next-preview
  candidates for labeling; `verify --hand` overlays the next-card match.
- Evolved Tesla's next-preview template was recovered from existing recordings
  via its unique purple evolution frame (it needs a separate template from the
  regular Tesla).


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-07-25 (evening)  —  Rocket -> Tornado combo
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Added a rocket+tornado combo to the reward shaping (env.py / config.yaml):
    * A tornado cast at the SAME spot right after a rocket that killed a clumped
      group is treated as one play.
    * It WAIVES the enemy-king penalty when that rocket hit a princess tower and
      killed >=2 medium troops (so a legit tower combo isn't discouraged).
    * It is rewarded for wiping a push (scaled by the enemy mass removed),
      anywhere on the board — not only next to a tower.
- Detection knobs: rewards.rocket_tornado_combo, env.combo_window_steps /
  combo_radius / combo_kill_min.
- Caveat: the training env evaluates each spell in a blocking window through its
  flight, so it can't cast the tornado mid-rocket-flight; the reward teaches the
  rocket->tornado PATTERN + the troops the rocket killed. The live bot performs
  the actual sub-second timing.
- (tooling, same day) train-rl now records a FIXED-LENGTH training timelapse
  (data/timelapses/timelapse_<date_time>.mp4 -- one timestamped file per run):
  however long training runs, the mp4 is always timelapse_seconds long at
  timelapse_fps (default 30 s @ 30 fps = 900 frames). Frames are thinned in memory
  during the run and resampled to exactly the target count on save. Not a
  model/reward change. See clashrl/timelapse.py + config train.timelapse*.
- Reduced defensive hesitancy. The RL play/wait balance had learned to WAIT (the
  small patience reward + avoiding play penalties made sitting still locally safe),
  and troop placements picked in the enemy half couldn't deploy at all (the card
  tap selects but the arena tap can't place -> "shuffling"). Fixes: penalise idling
  while a real enemy push is on the board (rewards.idle_penalty, env.threat_mass);
  clamp TROOP placements to your half of the river so they actually deploy, spells
  still target anywhere (action.deploy_top + ActionSpace.deploy_clamp, applied in
  env + play); and make training exploration commit to plays more (lower
  train.explore_wait_prob / min_play_elixir).
- Placement rule refined + elixir awareness (ARCHITECTURE CHANGE). (1) Only ROCKET
  and TORNADO may be cast ANYWHERE; every other card -- troops, buildings, AND
  Royal Delivery -- is now clamped to your half of the river (deploy_clamp keys off
  rocket/tornado, no longer off "is a spell"). (2) The elixir counter is now a
  POLICY INPUT: the normalized current elixir (0-1, read off the bar, ported from
  trol) is fed to the network via a new elixir_fc branch alongside the arena image,
  hand, and next card -- so the bot can learn elixir conservation. Threaded through
  the env, live play, dataset labeling, and BC + RL training. (3) Sitting on a FULL
  elixir bar with an enemy push and playing nothing now costs elixir_waste_penalty.
  The elixir input changes the model, so it needs a re-label + retrain
  (label --all -> train-bc -> train-rl).
- Faster match transitions + rocket-cycle incentives. (1) nav.menu_delay 1.0 -> 0.5
  so the bot doesn't idle on the home screen after a match before hitting Battle
  (the delay was the post-menu-tap sleep). (2) Rocket-cycle shaping: launching a
  rocket at an enemy princess tower now earns rocket_tower_reward (+ the troop combo
  if it also catches a clump), and playing a cheap card while the rocket ISN'T in
  hand earns cycle_reward -- together these teach the deck's cycle-to-rocket plan
  (chip the tower when safe; defend + the rocket->tornado combo when a big push
  comes) and give the still-hesitant policy clear positive things to do. Reward-only
  (no retrain needed beyond the pending elixir-input rebuild).
- 1v1 migration. (1) The Battle button now auto-queues, so the PARTY / quick-match
  step was removed from both nav loops (env.reset + play). (2) Deck card levels are
  recorded per card in cards.yaml (1v1 levels vary: e.g. Evo Tesla 14, Rocket 13,
  Skeletons 13, Ice Wizard 11); CardDB scales the level-11 base stats to the
  configured level (deck() + a level() accessor). Card stats don't drive the vision
  / RL rewards, so this is informational -- it doesn't change how the bot plays.
  (3) Towers recalibrated for 1v1 from a real recording: nudged the tower anchors
  and repositioned the princess HP-number boxes, and set hp_full to a level-14
  princess. IMPORTANT: the digit OCR (hp_digits.npz) was trained on 2v2 level-11
  towers and MISREADS the 1v1 (level 14-15) tower HP, so hp_reward is DISABLED for
  now to avoid injecting false chip rewards -- re-enable after retraining the digit
  model on 1v1 samples. Tower destruction (crowns) + win/loss still drive the reward.
- 1v1 match-end loop + tower reader RETRAINED (defence/offence chip re-enabled).
  (1) The 1v1 results screen puts "Play Again" (bottom-left) and "OK" (bottom-right)
  in new spots; the bot now taps PLAY AGAIN by default (buttons.play_again) from both
  nav loops (env.reset + play) and the RL results-read, so it re-queues straight into
  the next match without a detour through HOME.
  (2) Retrained the tower-HP digit CNN for 1v1. First fixed the HP-number boxes: the
  enemy boxes were clipping the last digit, and the "white" digits on YOUR blue bars
  are blue-tinted, so tower_hp._digit_band's strict mask was relaxed to catch them
  (the main white mask was already fine -- do NOT loosen it). Then labelled 47 HP
  strips from a real 1v1 recording (tools/hp_ocr/labeled_digits_1v1.npz) and retrained
  hp_digits.npz (2v2 base for digit coverage + 1v1 upweighted x3; ~97% per-digit). All
  four princess towers now read correctly -- verified end-to-end with clean, monotonic
  chip timelines (e.g. an enemy princess 4032->3499->2966->2553->1727).
  (3) Re-enabled hp_reward, and gave YOUR princess towers their own full HP
  (env.my_hp_full 4424, ~level 15) separate from the enemy's (env.hp_full 4032,
  ~level 14) so defence chip is seeded + normalised on the correct baseline. The
  reward change takes effect on the next train-rl.
- Reactive-play PERCEPTION + replay analysis (foresight groundwork). New enemy-threat
  reader (clashrl/threats.py): from a frame it classifies the incoming push by COLOUR
  (saturated green = Goblins, since this deck plays none; purple; neutral), SIZE (one
  big unit vs a swarm), COUNT, LANE and DEPTH over an "engagement zone" (midfield + your
  half, with the enemy back line, YOUR towers and the arena border masked out so only a
  real push registers), plus APPROACH SPEED and a best-effort PROJECTILE-IN-FLIGHT
  detector (a small blob tracked over several frames on a fast, near-straight arc toward
  a tower -- e.g. a thrown Goblin Barrel). It exposes a 14-value feature vector (for the
  policy, next phase) and a readable label. New `analyze` command (clashrl/analyze.py):
  for every card play in your recordings it reads the threat in the window before the
  play and aggregates WHICH CARD you play against WHICH threat type, and WHERE (on /
  behind the threat, your king, the enemy tower, ...), writing data/analysis/patterns.json
  + a report. On the latest session it recovered real habits: rocket -> enemy princess,
  ice wizard placed BEHIND the push, tesla ON the push, tornado onto your king, cheap
  cycle cards on/behind the threat; ~76% of plays are reactions. New `verify --threats`
  overlay to eyeball + tune the reader. ANALYSIS ONLY -- no model/reward change yet. Next:
  feed the threat vector into PolicyNet + reward the key foresight counters (Royal Delivery
  onto a threatened tower; tornado pulling a landed goblin swarm to your king) -- that step
  needs a re-label + retrain.
- Reactive-play PHASE 2 -- enemy-threat vector as a POLICY INPUT + foresight reward shaping.
  (1) ARCHITECTURE: PolicyNet gains a threat branch (threat_fc; embed 256+32+16+8 -> +16 =
  328). The 14-value threat vector (colour / size / count / lane / depth / speed + projectile,
  from clashrl.threats) is fed alongside the image / hand / next-card / elixir and threaded
  end-to-end: the labeler writes a `threats` array into dataset.npz, BC trains on it, and the
  live env + live play + the RL replay/DQN all carry it -- so behaviour-cloning learns your
  card-vs-threat habits and RL keeps + sharpens them. Back-compatible (old datasets/checkpoints
  default to zero threats). (2) FORESIGHT REWARDS (env): Royal Delivery dropped ON a landing
  goblin (green) swarm -- or onto the tower a projectile (Goblin Barrel) is arcing toward --
  earns rewards.threat_counter_delivery; a Tornado by YOUR king while a goblin swarm is present
  earns rewards.threat_tornado_pull (pull them into the king to tank + kill). Both are keyed off
  the live ThreatTracker read at action-selection time. Takes effect after the FULL rebuild:
  `label --all` -> `train-bc` -> (delete data/policy_rl.pt) -> `train-rl`. NOTE: the threat
  thresholds are tuned to the current (clean) arena; recordings on the older red-tint arena give
  noisier threat features.
- Reactive-play: behind-the-bridge SIEGE detection + counter. Buildings (X-Bow, Mortar) and
  troops (Princess) chip your towers from the ENEMY side WITHOUT crossing, so the your-half
  signals (my_side_mass / depth) read them as low threat. threats.py now measures `behind_mass`
  (enemy red in the band just above the river) and flags `siege` when that mass PERSISTS and does
  NOT advance/cross into your half over several reads -- a walking push advances (its centroid
  moves toward you) and crosses (my_side_mass rises), breaking the streak, while a siege sits so
  the streak builds. Wired in: the threat vector grows THREAT_DIM 14 -> 16 (behind_mass + siege)
  so the policy conditions on it (BC-loader pads/truncates old-width arrays for safety); the
  `analyze` report + patterns.json + `verify --threats` overlay surface it (a "siege-<lane>" label
  + an orange band/marker); and env rewards rocketing a sitting siege with rewards.siege_counter
  (the rocket is the only card that crosses the river to reach it). Takes effect after the full
  rebuild (label --all -> train-bc -> del data/policy_rl.pt -> train-rl). NOTE: the siege
  thresholds (_BEHIND_MIN / _SIEGE_*) in threats.py are best-effort; tune with verify --threats.
- GRADUAL own-tower penalty (better per-play credit). Losing HP on YOUR princess tower is now
  penalised PROPORTIONALLY as it's chipped -- accumulating up to |rewards.lose_own_tower| (-3)
  per tower and topped up to exactly that on destruction -- instead of a flat -3 only when the
  tower fell. So a play that concedes chip is penalised at that moment (easier for the DQN's
  per-step credit assignment to attribute), and a fully-lost princess still totals -3.
  tower_hp.TowerHpTracker owns the gradual charge (_update_my, capped + accumulated in
  _my_applied, in absolute units -- no longer x hp_scale x hp_defense_scale, which is retired);
  env tops it up on a princess fall via on_my_tower_destroyed(i); reward.TowerTracker now only
  emits the flat lose penalty for the KING (princesses are gradual, so no double-count). Verified
  a fully-lost princess = exactly -3.000 (gradual), and a burst-without-HP-reads = -3.0 (fallback).
  Reward change -> effective on the next train-rl.
- Rocket as an ELIXIR INVESTMENT (deferred / conditional chip). A rocket's chip reward (its
  rewards.rocket_tower_reward + the enemy-tower HP chip it deals) is no longer paid on impact --
  it is DEFERRED and only paid out if a SUCCESSFUL DEFENCE follows. If your towers lose
  >= env.rocket_bad_heavy_frac (0.33) of a tower's HP, or a princess falls, within
  env.rocket_defense_window (3) steps after the rocket, the chip is WITHHELD and an extra
  rewards.bad_rocket_penalty (-3) is applied -- so a rocket that leaves you unable to defend
  (a bad 6-elixir investment) is punished instead of rewarded, and chip damage only pays when
  the defence holds. Implemented in env (a pending-rocket state that folds the rocket's delayed
  HP chip -- tower_hp.last_enemy_chip -- into the deferred amount, then resolves: pay on a clean
  window, withhold + penalise on heavy damage / a felled princess). The learner is a per-step
  DQN, so this delayed reward is bootstrapped back onto the rocket via the value function.
  Reward change -> effective on the next train-rl.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Notes / caveats
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Reward-shaping changes only take effect after an RL run (`train-rl`) — they
  steer learning; they are not hard-coded behaviour.
- Card recognition is template matching (no learned classifier), so it needs
  per-deck templates built from your own recordings (`hand-templates`).
- Model weights (data/*.pt) and recordings (data/sessions/) are intentionally
  NOT committed — they are large and regenerable. Retrain to reproduce.
