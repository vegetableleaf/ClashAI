2026-07-25 (morning)  —  RL infrastructure, the policy model, reward shaping
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tower & King HP reader: a small CNN digit OCR (tower_hp.py + hp_digits.npz)
  reads tower/king HP off the screen to drive reward shaping.
- Action space + grid placement helper (actions.py); menu-navigation
  diagnostics (diagnose.py, the `diag` command).
- POLICY MODEL (model.py, PolicyNet): a shared CNN trunk over the downscaled
  arena image, concatenated with a hand multi-hot, feeding two heads:
    * card head  — which card IDENTITY to play (an evolved card is its own
      identity, since it plays differently — not the tray slot, which cycles)
    * cell head  — which grid cell to place it on
- Behaviour cloning (train_bc.py) pretrains the policy to copy your recorded
  plays (card head trained with a mask over the cards actually in hand).
- RL fine-tune (train_rl.py): a DQN wrapper adds a wait/play GATE head over the
  shared embedding; target network + replay; epsilon-greedy with elixir-aware
  exploration.
- Live RL environment (env.py) + reward shaping (reward.py):
    * +take enemy tower, -lose own tower, +win / -loss
    * defend-your-towers (tower-HP based), troop-defeat + patience
      (enemy-mass detection)
    * spell rewards scaled by the troops HIT / KILLED; Rocket auto-aims the
      weaker enemy princess tower
    * range-aware, center-biased DEFENSIVE placement for troop cards
    * tornado-onto-your-own-king "tank / activate" reward (king-HP based)

2026-07-25 (evening)  —  Rocket -> Tornado combo
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Added a rocket+tornado combo to the reward shaping (env.py / config.yaml):
    * A tornado cast at the SAME spot right after a rocket that killed a clumped
      group is treated as one play.
    * It WAIVES the enemy-king penalty when that rocket hit a princess tower and
      killed >=2 medium troops (so a legit tower combo isn't discouraged).
    * It is rewarded for wiping a push (scaled by the enemy mass removed),
      anywhere on the board — not only next to a tower.
- Detection knobs: rewards.rocket_tornado_combo, env.combo_window_steps /
  combo_radius / combo_kill_min.
- Caveat: the training env evaluates each spell in a blocking window through its
  flight, so it can't cast the tornado mid-rocket-flight; the reward teaches the
  rocket->tornado PATTERN + the troops the rocket killed. The live bot performs
  the actual sub-second timing.
- (tooling, same day) train-rl now records a FIXED-LENGTH training timelapse
  (data/timelapses/timelapse_<date_time>.mp4 -- one timestamped file per run):
  however long training runs, the mp4 is always timelapse_seconds long at
  timelapse_fps (default 30 s @ 30 fps = 900 frames). Frames are thinned in memory
  during the run and resampled to exactly the target count on save. Not a
  model/reward change. See clashrl/timelapse.py + config train.timelapse*.
- Reduced defensive hesitancy. The RL play/wait balance had learned to WAIT (the
  small patience reward + avoiding play penalties made sitting still locally safe),

  still target anywhere (action.deploy_top + ActionSpace.deploy_clamp, applied in

  ice wizard placed BEHIND the push, tesla ON the push, tornado onto your king, cheap
  cycle cards on/behind the threat; ~76% of plays are reactions. New `verify --threats`
  overlay to eyeball + tune the reader. ANALYSIS ONLY -- no model/reward change yet. Next:
  feed the threat vector into PolicyNet + reward the key foresight counters (Royal Delivery
  onto a threatened tower; tornado pulling a landed goblin swarm to your king) -- that step
  needs a re-label + retrain.
- Reactive-play PHASE 2 -- enemy-threat vector as a POLICY INPUT + foresight reward shaping.
  (1) ARCHITECTURE: PolicyNet gains a threat branch (threat_fc; embed 256+32+16+8 -> +16 =
  328). The 14-value threat vector (colour / size / count / lane / depth / speed + projectile,
  from clashrl.threats) is fed alongside the image / hand / next-card / elixir and threaded
  end-to-end: the labeler writes a `threats` array into dataset.npz, BC trains on it, and the
  live env + live play + the RL replay/DQN all carry it -- so behaviour-cloning learns your
  card-vs-threat habits and RL keeps + sharpens them. Back-compatible (old datasets/checkpoints
  default to zero threats). (2) FORESIGHT REWARDS (env): Royal Delivery dropped ON a landing
  goblin (green) swarm -- or onto the tower a projectile (Goblin Barrel) is arcing toward --
  earns rewards.threat_counter_delivery; a Tornado by YOUR king while a goblin swarm is present
  earns rewards.threat_tornado_pull (pull them into the king to tank + kill). Both are keyed off
  the live ThreatTracker read at action-selection time. Takes effect after the FULL rebuild:
  `label --all` -> `train-bc` -> (delete data/policy_rl.pt) -> `train-rl`. NOTE: the threat
  thresholds are tuned to the current (clean) arena; recordings on the older red-tint arena give
  noisier threat features.
- Reactive-play: behind-the-bridge SIEGE detection + counter. Buildings (X-Bow, Mortar) and
  troops (Princess) chip your towers from the ENEMY side WITHOUT crossing, so the your-half
  signals (my_side_mass / depth) read them as low threat. threats.py now measures `behind_mass`
  (enemy red in the band just above the river) and flags `siege` when that mass PERSISTS and does
  NOT advance/cross into your half over several reads -- a walking push advances (its centroid
  moves toward you) and crosses (my_side_mass rises), breaking the streak, while a siege sits so
  the streak builds. Wired in: the threat vector grows THREAT_DIM 14 -> 16 (behind_mass + siege)
  so the policy conditions on it (BC-loader pads/truncates old-width arrays for safety); the
  `analyze` report + patterns.json + `verify --threats` overlay surface it (a "siege-<lane>" label
  + an orange band/marker); and env rewards rocketing a sitting siege with rewards.siege_counter
  (the rocket is the only card that crosses the river to reach it). Takes effect after the full
  rebuild (label --all -> train-bc -> del data/policy_rl.pt -> train-rl). NOTE: the siege
  thresholds (_BEHIND_MIN / _SIEGE_*) in threats.py are best-effort; tune with verify --threats.
- GRADUAL own-tower penalty (better per-play credit). Losing HP on YOUR princess tower is now
  penalised PROPORTIONALLY as it's chipped -- accumulating up to |rewards.lose_own_tower| (-3)
  per tower and topped up to exactly that on destruction -- instead of a flat -3 only when the
  tower fell. So a play that concedes chip is penalised at that moment (easier for the DQN's
  per-step credit assignment to attribute), and a fully-lost princess still totals -3.
  tower_hp.TowerHpTracker owns the gradual charge (_update_my, capped + accumulated in
  _my_applied, in absolute units -- no longer x hp_scale x hp_defense_scale, which is retired);
  env tops it up on a princess fall via on_my_tower_destroyed(i); reward.TowerTracker now only
  emits the flat lose penalty for the KING (princesses are gradual, so no double-count). Verified
  a fully-lost princess = exactly -3.000 (gradual), and a burst-without-HP-reads = -3.0 (fallback).
  Reward change -> effective on the next train-rl.
- Rocket as an ELIXIR INVESTMENT (deferred / conditional chip). A rocket's chip reward (its
  rewards.rocket_tower_reward + the enemy-tower HP chip it deals) is no longer paid on impact --
  it is DEFERRED and only paid out if a SUCCESSFUL DEFENCE follows. If within
  env.rocket_defense_window (3) steps your towers take MORE HP damage than the rocket dealt the
  enemy tower (compared in HP: your towers' HP lost vs the rocket's measured enemy-tower chip),
  or a princess falls, the chip is WITHHELD and an extra rewards.bad_rocket_penalty (-3) is
  applied -- so a rocket that trades DOWN (you lose more than you gain) is punished instead of
  rewarded, and chip damage only pays when the defence holds. Implemented in env (a pending-rocket
  state that folds the rocket's delayed HP chip + measured tower damage -- tower_hp.last_enemy_chip
  / last_enemy_frac -- into the deferred amount, then resolves: pay on a good trade, withhold +
  penalise on own-damage > rocket-damage / a felled princess). The learner is a per-step DQN, so
  this delayed reward is bootstrapped back onto the rocket via the value function.
  Reward change -> effective on the next train-rl.

2026-07-26  —  Anti-hesitation: potential-based troop reward + reweighting
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BUG: a hesitant agent could END a match NET POSITIVE (+7.7) while being
  three-crowned (a loss). Root cause was reward-SHAPE, not just magnitudes: the
  per-step troop-defeat reward used a RECTIFIED delta -- max(0, prev_mass - cur_mass)
  -- so it paid out on every DOWN-swing of the noisy enemy-mass signal (troops
  ebbing / dying at the towers, occlusion) but never charged the matching UP-swings.
  Summed over a ~150-step match this drift, plus a standing +patience per idle step,
  banked more than the -10 loss. It fired even on pure no-op steps, so DOING NOTHING
  was literally profitable.
- FIX (env.py): the troop term is now POTENTIAL-BASED -- the SIGNED per-step change
  in on-board enemy mass, clipped to +/-defeat_cap x rewards.troop_defeat. Being
  symmetric it telescopes over the match (board starts ~empty and, in a loss, ends
  non-empty -> the term is <= ~0 for a loss), so idling can't farm it and a growing
  push is penalised immediately. Removed the asymmetric clean_kill_bonus x2 from this
  term (it re-opened the same farm); killing troops before they reach your tower is
  still favoured because tower chip + tower loss are the (larger) negatives.
- REWEIGHTING (config.yaml) to guarantee a loss is always net-negative and acting
  beats stalling: patience 0.02 -> 0.0 (a quiet board is NEUTRAL, not rewarded);
  idle_penalty -0.3 -> -0.5 (bigger than any single-step positive shaping);
  loss -10 -> -12 (dominates residual shaping); env.threat_mass 0.10 -> 0.06 (the
  idle penalty engages on smaller pushes); clean_kill_bonus 2.0 -> 1.0 (deprecated).
- Net effect: a three-crown loss now nets clearly negative (loss + own-tower
  penalties + a <=0 telescoped troop term), and the standing incentive to sit idle is
  gone. Reward-shaping change -> effective on the next train-rl (consider deleting
  data/policy_rl.pt so the wait/play gate re-learns from the BC start).

2026-07-26  —  Save the Tesla for the enemy's win condition (tower-targeter)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Goal: if the enemy runs a WIN CONDITION (a troop that heads straight for your tower and
  ignores your troops -- Hog / Ram / Balloon / Giant / Royal Giant / Miner ...), HOLD the
  Tesla and only deploy it to defend that win condition; don't spend it early. If the enemy
  has NO win condition, the Tesla plays defensively as normal.
- PERCEPTION (threats.py ThreatTracker): a "win condition" is detected BEHAVIOURALLY (no
  enemy-card ID is possible from the vision) -- an enemy unit that PENETRATES deep into your
  half (depth >= env.wc_depth, 0 at the river .. 1 at your king) OR CROSSES fast toward it
  (speed >= env.wc_fast_speed), with a real unit present (my_side_mass >= env.wc_min_mass).
  Per match: seeing one latches `enemy_has_wc`; `wc_active` tracks one being on the board
  (wc_confirm reads to arm, wc_release quiet reads to clear -- hysteresis). New helper
  `should_hold_tesla() = enemy_has_wc and not wc_active`.
- BEHAVIOUR: live play (play.py) MASKS the Tesla out of the card choice while should_hold_tesla
  (it waits if the Tesla is the only card) -> the Tesla is saved. Training (env.py) REWARD-SHAPES
  the same policy: +rewards.wc_tesla_defend for playing Tesla against an ACTIVE win condition,
  rewards.tesla_hold_penalty for spending it early when one is known but off the board.
- Honest limits (told user): behavioural, so the FIRST win condition of a match can't be known
  in advance (it latches on first sighting, then the Tesla is saved for the recurring cycles);
  a deep undefended non-WC push can latch it too (still sensible -- Tesla answers deep threats).
  Thresholds are coarse pixel proxies -- calibrate wc_depth / wc_fast_speed with `verify --threats`
  (a red "WIN-COND" label marks a per-frame detection).
- config: rewards.wc_tesla_defend / tesla_hold_penalty; env.wc_depth / wc_fast_speed / wc_min_mass
  / wc_confirm / wc_release. Live gate is immediate; the reward shaping needs a train-rl run.

2026-07-26  —  Centre defensive placement is a RECOMMENDATION, not a mandate
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- User: the centre placement is NOT a must -- it's a recommended default; in some cases other
  placements are better (block a lane so a troop can't reach your tower; drop a Ronin up front
  to catch an enemy ranged unit off guard). Let the model choose.
- Before: the range-aware centre spot was a HARD OVERRIDE -- env.step and play.act REPLACED the
  model's chosen cell for Tesla / Ice Wizard / Ronin whenever a threat was up, so the model could
  never place them anywhere else.
- Now: the override is REMOVED (env + live play). The model's own placement stands (still passed
  through deploy_clamp -> your half + off your king, unchanged). Centre is kept as a SOFT DEFAULT
  via a small reward: env._recommended_defense_cell (was _place_defensive) computes the suggested
  centre spot and env._defense_placement_reward gives rewards.defense_center_bonus (0.15) when the
  model places within a cell of it -- and NOTHING otherwise (no penalty for placing elsewhere). A
  better lane block / forward play earns more through the troop-defeat + Tesla-kill rewards, so the
  model deviates from centre when that's stronger.
- play.py: dropped the defensive-override branch + its now-dead setup (CardDB/_db, defensive_kind,
  defense_params, threat_min_frac, and the defensive_cell/threat_front/threat_side imports); kept
  the Tesla win-condition hold gate and the rocket weaker-princess aim. config: rewards.defense_center_bonus.
- Live effect is immediate (the override is gone); the centre-default nudge shapes the policy on
  the next train-rl. The earlier deploy_clamp king-avoidance still keeps every placement deployable.

2026-07-26  —  Object detection (YOLO11) — Stage 1: dataset bootstrap
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direction: replace the coarse red-blob threat proxies with real per-unit OBJECT DETECTION so
  the policy has a robust "what troop/spell is where" read (the model was "guessing every turn").
  Chosen: Ultralytics YOLO11 (over vanilla DETR -- far more trainable on limited, custom data;
  RT-DETR is a drop-in later since the dataset format is identical). ~55-73 class taxonomy.
- Stage 1 (this change) = the DATA pipeline, since labels are the real bottleneck:
  * config/detect_classes.yaml -- ordered class taxonomy (your deck + common ladder cards +
    spells), team-agnostic (team inferred from colour at inference).
  * clashrl/detect.py + `run.py autolabel [--all] [--preview]` -- builds an Ultralytics dataset
    under data/detect (images/ + labels/ + data.yaml). AUTO-boxes YOUR OWN just-spawned troops
    for free: reuses label._extract_plays (your select+placement clicks) -> a known class at a
    known tap; the unit that appears is localised by a frame-diff around the tap (unions near-tap
    blobs so a Skeletons group boxes as one). Also exports extra active in-match frames (sampled
    AROUND your plays -- detect_state is a weak in-match positive, reads most in-match frames as
    UNKNOWN, so it's NOT used) with empty labels for you to hand-label the ENEMY on.
  * tools/detect/train.py -- Ultralytics trainer (yolo11n, imgsz 960, HSV jitter so blue-troop
    labels transfer to the red enemy side, no h-flip since lanes are asymmetric). Needs
    `pip install ultralytics`.
  * config.yaml detect: section (spawn_delay/search_radius/diff_thresh/general_per_session/...).
- VALIDATED on a real session: 26 plays -> 19 accurate own-troop boxes (Ronin/Ice Wizard/Ice
  Spirit/Skeletons) + 40 frames to hand-label; data.yaml valid (nc + names). Preview overlay
  confirmed the auto boxes land on the spawned unit. data/detect is gitignored (regenerable).
- CAVEAT (told user): auto labels only cover YOUR troops and a frame also holds enemy + other
  own units -- must hand-label those on each frame (a few hundred) before training, else the
  detector learns them as background. NEXT stages: (2) hand-label seed + active-learning + colour
  transfer for the enemy; (3) detections -> semantic-map channels into PolicyNet; (4) retrain BC+RL.

2026-07-27  —  Stage 4 groundwork: external-replay strategy mining (deferred, detector-gated)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Motivation (user): the user isn't a pro, so BC from only their own play caps out. Idea: have the
  bot learn from strong ROCKET-CYCLE players' replays -- their defensive placements, positive elixir
  trades, and when they rocket the enemy tower. Assessed frame-by-frame BC from online footage as the
  WRONG vehicle (hard blockers: external video has NO mouse log for labels; the whole vision stack --
  card templates / elixir / tower / HP OCR -- is calibrated to the user's ONE rendering; pixel-BC
  suffers domain shift across renderings; and a different "rocket cycle" deck doesn't match this deck's
  card-identity action space). Chosen instead: STRATEGY DISTILLATION -- mine footage into compact,
  DECK-AGNOSTIC priors that reward shaping leans on (the channel by which strategy already enters this
  bot), not pixel cloning.
- Built the groundwork now (the parts that don't need the detector), so it's ready to point at footage
  the moment Stage 2/3 lands (clashrl/replay_mine.py, `run.py mine-replays`):
    * DETECTOR PLUG-POINT: BoardDetector / load_detector wrap the SAME Ultralytics YOLO/RT-DETR weights
      as detect-preview (runs/detect/*/weights/best.pt); team (mine/enemy) inferred from colour tint.
      `.available` is False with no weights, so the miner reports READINESS + next steps instead of
      crashing (verified: it prints the Stage-3 dependency and staging instructions).
    * PRIOR SCHEMA (versioned JSON, schema_version 1): StrategyPriors aggregates placements
      [threat_bucket][card][zone], elixir TRADES [threat_bucket][card] (n / delta-sum / positive), and
      ROCKET-TOWER launch conditions (elixir histogram + board quiet-vs-pressured). Threat is bucketed
      DECK-AGNOSTICALLY via card_threat (win_condition / siege / tank / air / swarm / spell / support),
      which is what lets the priors transfer across decks.
    * REWARD HOOKS (pure, ready for env at Stage 4): placement_bonus / trade_bonus / rocket_opportunity
      each return ~[0,1]; empty priors (no data/file) return 0, so they're safe by construction. GATED

    * MINER: mine_replays runs the detector over videos in replay_mine.replays_dir (data/replays),

Notes / caveats
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Reward-shaping changes only take effect after an RL run (`train-rl`) — they
  steer learning; they are not hard-coded behaviour.

  per-deck templates built from your own recordings (`hand-templates`).
