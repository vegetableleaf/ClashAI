# R2 Owner Batch Review — sim-parity sweep, 2026-08-26

One merged view of eleven independent R2 claim files (8 family sweeps + 3 cross-checks), deduped by `(key, field)`. Canonical ledger: `research/sim_parity/ledger/stat_diffs.jsonl` (**556** rows). Nothing in `icebow/config/cards.yaml` or `cards_stats.json` was modified — this is a proposal sheet.

**Dedupe rule applied.** Where two sweeps claimed the same `(key, field)`, the claim with more sources was kept and the loser is preserved verbatim under `dup_verdicts` in the canonical row. Where duplicates disagreed — different verdict, different proposed value, or a different reading of `current_db` — the merged verdict was forced to `escalate` (section 2a). **39** pairs were duplicated: **26** agreed, **13** did not.

**How to read a verdict.** `update` = two or more paths agree the KB is wrong and no curated `verified: true` value is being overturned. `escalate` = a human must decide. `pin` = the KB is right and the wiki is wrong, recorded so a later scrape does not "fix" it. `match` = recorded agreement, kept because it corrects an earlier claim.
