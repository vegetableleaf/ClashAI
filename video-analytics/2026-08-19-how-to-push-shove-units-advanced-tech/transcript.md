Mmm, it's an orange juice. Hey guys, this is Jan from Orange Juice. Today I'll be talking about a basic game mechanic. Pushing units.

You can push slower units with faster units. Here the hog pushes the giant skeleton. And as a result, he pushes the tower faster which allows him to get an extra hidden. To understand how this mechanic works, let's break it down. A hog is a very fast moving unit. A giant skeleton is a medium speed unit. A mini pecca is a fast moving unit. The very fast hog and the medium speed giant skeleton averaged out to be fast. It's a compromise but it makes sense to execute this with certain combos.

The dragon shoves the balloon to the tower but one second faster. It's not going to get an extra attack in but that one second is devastating with a balloon. If you're too eager with your timing and plant a balloon too close, it'll stumble with the dragon.

Alternatively, you can push very fast units with other very fast units. The hog has a one second deploy time. During this deploy time you can shove the hog with melee goblins. It's not going to win you the game but every little detail adds up. You must have good technique where this will backfire on you. Planting the hogs too late will cause the goblin to run in front of the hog. One second doesn't seem like much but that's one second less for your opponent to react.

Here's a concrete example demonstrating just how useful this technique truly is. The archer locked into the tower one second earlier whereas if she didn't get pushed, she would have targeted the barbarians. She ended up doing over 300 damage to the tower. Not bad considering most people would have just let her die.
